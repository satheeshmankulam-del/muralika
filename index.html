Meet your teacher

Gayathri S N

Carnatic Musician, Devotional Singer & Teacher

With a deep love for Carnatic music and devotion, I founded Muralika School of Music to share the beauty of traditional Indian music with students of all ages.

My musical journey has included devotional performances at renowned sacred destinations, including Mookambika Temple, Guruvayur Temple, Attukal Devi Temple, Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple and Kuttikadu Devi Temple.

"I am especially blessed to perform at Mookambika and Guruvayur every year, continuing a cherished musical and devotional tradition."

At Muralika, students learn through listening, repetition, dedicated practice and guided singing. Our aim is to nurture every student's musical ability, devotion, confidence and appreciation for the rich tradition of Carnatic music.

Featured performance · 23 December 2025

Vande Padmanabham at Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

ശ്രീ പത്മനാഭസ്വാമി ക്ഷേത്രം · ലക്ഷദീപം മുറജപം 2025

During the Lakshadeepam (Murajapam) festival of 2025, the students of Muralika School of Music presented a Carnatic music concert at Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, Thiruvananthapuram. It was part of Vande Padmanabham, the 56-day arts and cultural programme at East Nada.

Children and adults sang together on the festival stage, and Gayathri S N was honoured on behalf of the school.
